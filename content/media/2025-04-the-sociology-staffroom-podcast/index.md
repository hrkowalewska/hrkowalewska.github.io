abstract: |-
  Sociology lecturer at the University of Bath, Helen Kowalewska, joins Katie in the Sociology
  Staffroom to discuss her research into the impact of social policy on women and gender
  inequality. They discuss Helen’s most recent research into women over the age of 60 (including
  the WASPI women) and the different research methods that she has employed. They talk about the
  often double-edged nature of social policy that can on the one hand help women but in other
  respects reinforce patriarchal assumptions (e.g. maternity leave). They also consider some of
  the reasons for why there has not been more progress towards gender equality.
